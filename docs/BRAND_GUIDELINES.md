# Qarar Brand Guidelines

## Brand decision

Keep the name **Qarar | قرار**. It is direct, memorable in Arabic, easy to explain in English, and accurately describes the product without AI hype.

## Slogan candidates

1. **قرارات أوضح. بثقة أكبر. / Decide Better.** — recommended: short, human and works for personal and enterprise decisions.
2. **فكّر بوضوح. قرر بثقة. / Think Clearly. Decide Confidently.** — strong but slightly instructional.
3. **من التعقيد إلى قرار واضح. / From Complexity to a Clear Decision.** — descriptive but longer.
4. **شوف الصورة كاملة. / See the Whole Picture.** — approachable, less formal.
5. **افهم أكثر. قرر أفضل. / Understand More. Decide Better.** — clear but generic.

Primary slogan: **قرارات أوضح. بثقة أكبر.**  
English: **Decide Better.**  
Secondary tagline: **من التعقيد إلى قرار واضح. / From complexity to a clear decision.**

## Pitch

Qarar helps people and organizations understand important decisions, compare options and risks, and move from evidence to an explainable human-approved action.

Arabic description: قرار منصة ذكية تجمع السياق والأدلة والبدائل والمخاطر في مسار واضح، لتساعد الأفراد والفرق على فهم الصورة، مقارنة الخيارات، واعتماد قرارات قابلة للتفسير والتنفيذ والمتابعة.

English description: Qarar is a decision intelligence platform that organizes context, evidence, alternatives, and risks into a clear journey, helping individuals and teams compare options and make explainable, actionable decisions.

## Mission

Make high-quality decision thinking accessible without requiring users to understand analytics, AI architecture or decision-science terminology.

## Personality and voice

- Calm, intelligent, clear, modern and human.
- Confident about the process, never falsely certain about the answer.
- Saudi and regional in language quality, globally professional in product behavior.
- Arabic uses natural business language; English is written natively rather than translated literally.

## Visual system

- Deep emerald: trust and primary brand moments.
- Royal blue: actions and navigation.
- Purple: insight and evidence.
- Teal: opportunity.
- Amber: warning and pending decisions.
- Red: material risk only.
- Warm white/slate: primary reading surfaces.

Typography: IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic and Inter for English. Body copy should remain 14–18px; major headings should not dominate the viewport.

## Logo direction

A compact Arabic **ق** / Latin **Q** mark formed by a single decision path that enters, branches once, and exits with a selected direction. Avoid brains, robots, chat bubbles and generic sparkle symbols. Required future assets: icon, horizontal, dark, light and favicon variants.
